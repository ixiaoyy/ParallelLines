# Product

## Register

product

## Users

ParallelLines serves Chinese technical community members who read, post, reply, search, and manage forum discussions. Authenticated moderators and administrators use the product in focused operational contexts: reviewing content, maintaining boards, checking system health, managing users, and inspecting analytics.

## Product Purpose

ParallelLines is a Discourse-inspired forum for traceable technical answers and community discussion. Success means members can find reliable context, participate with low friction, and operators can keep the site healthy with transparent, permission-aware tools.

## Brand Personality

Calm, technical, trustworthy. The interface should feel bright, organized, and competent without turning operational surfaces into marketing pages.

## Anti-references

Avoid decorative SaaS dashboards, heavy purple gradients, glassmorphism, fake metrics, over-rounded cards, and page designs that hide real forum data behind generic illustration or oversized hero treatments.

## Design Principles

- Preserve trust by showing real data states and permission boundaries clearly.
- Favor scan-friendly density for repeat operational workflows.
- Keep brand color usage disciplined: Element UI blue for primary actions, semantic accents for state, and board tones for board identity.
- Let route pages orchestrate data while feature components own reusable interface sections.
- Make empty, loading, and error states explain what is happening without implying unavailable capabilities already exist.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Aim for readable contrast, visible focus states, keyboard-reachable controls, non-hover-only interactions, and reduced-motion-safe transitions. Chinese copy should stay concise and avoid internal implementation jargon unless the target user is an administrator reading an operational report.
